Insurance & Coverage FAQ

Q: What is the annual maximum benefit?
A: Most Delta Dental PPO plans have an annual maximum of $1,500 per person. Some enhanced plans offer up to $2,000.

Q: Does my plan cover orthodontic treatment?
A: Orthodontic coverage varies by plan. Most plans cover 50% of orthodontic costs up to a lifetime maximum of $1,500 for dependents under age 19.

Q: What is a waiting period?
A: A waiting period is the time you must be enrolled before certain services are covered. Basic restorative services typically have a 6-month waiting period, and major services may require 12 months.

Q: Are dental implants covered?
A: Implants are generally considered a major service and are covered at 50% after the waiting period, subject to the annual maximum. Pre-authorization is recommended.

Q: How do I find an in-network provider?
A: Visit the member portal at deltadental.com or call the number on your insurance card. Using an in-network provider maximizes your benefits and reduces out-of-pocket costs.

Q: What is a Coordination of Benefits (COB)?
A: If you are covered by more than one dental plan, COB determines which plan pays first (primary) and which pays second (secondary) to avoid duplicate payments.
