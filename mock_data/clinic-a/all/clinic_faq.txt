APPOINTMENTS

Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: You can book an appointment by calling us at (555) 834-2200, emailing info@smiledentalclinic.com, or through the patient portal.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: We recommend booking at least 3–5 business days in advance for routine visits. Emergency appointments are accommodated same-day when possible.

Q: How long is a typical appointment?
A: Routine cleanings are 60 minutes. Fillings and restorations are 60–90 minutes. Implant consultations are 45 minutes. Complex procedures may take longer.

Q: What should I bring to my first appointment?
A: Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, and any prior dental records or X-rays if available.

Q: Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
A: Yes. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may be subject to a $50 cancellation fee.

SERVICES

Q: What services does the clinic offer?
A: We offer a full range of dental services including:
- Preventive care: cleanings, exams, X-rays, fluoride treatments
- Restorative: fillings (composite and amalgam), crowns, bridges
- Cosmetic: teeth whitening, veneers
- Surgical: extractions, dental implants
- Orthodontic referrals

Q: Do you offer emergency dental services?
A: Yes. Call (555) 834-2200 during business hours for same-day emergency care. After-hours emergencies are directed to our on-call line.

Q: Do you offer teeth whitening?
A: Yes. We offer in-office whitening treatments and take-home whitening kits. Ask our staff for current pricing and availability.

INSURANCE & PAYMENTS

Q: Does the clinic accept dental insurance?
A: Yes. We accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental PPO, Aetna, Cigna, MetLife, and Blue Cross Blue Shield. Please call ahead to confirm your specific plan.

Q: What if I don't have insurance?
A: We offer a Self-Pay Discount Plan. Patients without insurance receive a 15% discount on all services paid at the time of treatment.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), and CareCredit financing.

Q: Do you offer payment plans?
A: Yes. We partner with CareCredit to offer 6-month and 12-month interest-free financing options for qualifying patients.

Q: How do I get a cost estimate before treatment?
A: Ask our front desk to request a pre-treatment estimate from your insurance provider. We are happy to submit this on your behalf.
